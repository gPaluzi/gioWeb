## Overview
In 2026, teams conducting the Javan Leopard Population Viability Analysis need deforestation rate data to simulate changes in habitat carrying capacity. This mini-app serves as an explorer for forest cover datasets available in the Google Earth Engine Data Catalog, helping users identify relevant datasets for their analysis.
