## Overview
Back when tally sheets were digitized from field records into formatted Excel files, the workflow worked well for small-scale operations. As data volume and complexity grew, Excel-based processing became difficult to maintain, scale, and validate consistently.

This ingestion layer is designed to transition data management into a relational database system, enabling scalable storage, structured validation, traceability, and downstream analytics.
